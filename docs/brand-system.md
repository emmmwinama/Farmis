# AgriVault Brand System

## Identity

AgriVault is a professional farm-record vault for field teams, managers, accountants, lenders, buyers, auditors, and insurers. The product should feel secure, clear, data-driven, and calm.

## Logo Rules

- Use the shield mark for app icons, favicons, compact navigation, and watermarks.
- Use the full AgriVault lockup in login, landing, reports, and wide navigation.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least half the shield width.
- Do not stretch, recolor into warm/brown palettes, add shadows behind the mark, or place it on low-contrast images.

## Palette

- Vault navy: `#0F172A` for sidebars, report covers, and high-emphasis text.
- Data blue: `#0284C7` for primary actions and active states.
- Cyan accent: `#06B6D4` for sync, offline, and progress states.
- Ice background: `#F8FAFC` for app pages.
- Sky surface: `#E0F2FE` for highlighted panels.
- Slate text: `#334155` for body copy.
- Success teal: `#0D9488` for positive financial signals.
- Risk red: `#DC2626` for destructive or loss states.

## Typography

- Use the app font stack already configured in Next.js.
- Page titles: 24-32px, 800-900 weight.
- Section headings: 14-18px, 800 weight.
- Body and form text: 14-16px, 500-700 weight.
- Avoid negative letter spacing in compact UI.

## Icons

- Use flat Lucide icons.
- Keep icons one color inside controls.
- Avoid emoji-style icons and animated decorative icons in the product UI.
- Use 16-20px icons for normal controls and 22-24px for large touch actions.

## Buttons And Touch Targets

- Primary actions use Data blue background with white text.
- Secondary actions use white or Ice background with slate text and a blue-tinted border.
- Minimum touch target: 44px height for buttons, selects, inputs, and icon buttons.
- Use destructive red only for delete/archive confirmations.

## Reports

- Report covers should use Vault navy, Data blue, farm name, date range, filters, and purpose.
- Use section dividers for Fields, Activities, Finance, Payroll, Livestock, and Analytics.
- Prefer tables for evidence and charts for summaries.
- Every PDF page should include page number, farm name, and generated date.
